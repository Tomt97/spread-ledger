How to get the thinkorswim CSV
  1. Open thinkorswim on a computer and go to Monitor → Account Statement.
  2. Pick the date range you want (you can import overlapping ranges; trades already imported are skipped).
  3. Open the menu at the top right of the statement and choose Export to File, then save the .csv.
  4. Here, choose Import thinkorswim CSV and pick that file. You'll see a preview before anything is saved.
